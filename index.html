Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

English Türkçe

This Privacy Policy describes how Konselit Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the FF BASE mobile application (package com.konselit.ffbase) for Android.

This policy is intended to explain our practices in plain language. It is not legal advice and does not create contractual guarantees beyond what is required by applicable law.

1. What the app does

FF BASE is a Free Fire companion app: character/weapon/pet catalog, loadout and compare tools, and a news / video / event feed. The mobile app does not require an account.

2. Information stored on your device

These items stay on your phone or tablet unless you delete the app or clear app data:

  • Favorites, saved packages/loadouts, and saved news
  • Theme preference (dark/light)
  • Which feed stories you have already seen
  • Cached feed and article text so the app can work offline

We do not receive a copy of this local data as a user profile.

3. Information processed online

To show current news, events, and official videos, the app may request publicly published content from our Supabase backend using a public (anon) API key. That request does not require your name, email, or a user account.

Opening a story may load publisher pages or images from third-party sites. Playing a video may use a YouTube embed (Google). Those services process data under their own policies.

4. Advertising

FF BASE displays advertisements to support the app. Advertising is delivered through third-party providers, which currently include:

  • Google AdMob (including App Open ads served directly in the app)
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource (including interstitial ads, native ads, and mediation)
  • Mediation networks that may participate through LevelPlay, such as Google AdMob and Unity Ads, depending on auction fill

These providers may process device, advertising identifiers (for example Advertising ID where permitted), interaction, diagnostic, and approximate location / network information according to their own policies and any applicable consent choices. We do not control every data practice of those providers.

For more detail, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads / LevelPlay privacy information

5. Consent and advertising choices

Where required by applicable law (for example in the EEA/UK), FF BASE or our ad partners may present a consent or privacy choices interface for advertising (such as Google User Messaging Platform). Your choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized ads are shown. Availability of specific controls can vary by region, device, and OS version.

6. How we use information

We use the information described in this policy to:

  • operate and improve FF BASE
  • deliver feed and catalog content
  • show ads and support mediation
  • diagnose technical issues and maintain security
  • respond to privacy or support requests

7. Sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information as a consumer profile. We use service providers to help run FF BASE, including hosting (Supabase), video embeds (YouTube/Google), and the advertising partners listed above. Advertising partners may collect or receive information independently under their own terms.

8. Retention

Local data remains until you remove it in the app or uninstall the app. Published feed content is stored on our backend for the public feed. Advertising partners retain data according to their own retention policies.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the data we process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Children

FF BASE is a game companion and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that is not permitted, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

11. Your rights and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, deletion, objection to certain processing, or withdrawal of consent where consent is the legal basis. To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. Some advertising or device-level choices may also need to be managed through your device settings or the advertising provider’s tools.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of FF BASE after an update means the revised policy applies to subsequent use, except where a different notice is required by law.

Gizlilik Politikası

Son güncelleme: 2 Ekim 2026

Bu politika, Konselit Studio’nun FF BASE mobil uygulamasında (com.konselit.ffbase) bilgiyi nasıl işlediğini açıklar. Bu metin hukuki tavsiye değildir.

1. Uygulama ne yapar

FF BASE bir Free Fire yardımcı uygulamasıdır: katalog, loadout / compare araçları ve haber / video / etkinlik akışı. Mobil uygulamada hesap açılmaz.

2. Cihazınızda tutulan bilgiler

Aşağıdakiler telefondadır; uygulamayı silmedikçe veya uygulama verisini temizlemedikçe kullanıcı profili olarak bizde kopyası tutulmaz:

  • Favoriler, kayıtlı paketler/loadout’lar ve kaydedilen haberler
  • Tema tercihi
  • Görülen feed kayıtları
  • Çevrimdışı kullanım için feed ve makale önbelleği

3. Çevrimiçi işlenen bilgiler

Güncel haber, etkinlik ve videolar için uygulama, Supabase üzerindeki yayınlanmış içeriği public (anon) anahtarla okuyabilir; ad, e-posta veya hesap göndermez. Video ve harici sayfalar YouTube / yayıncı siteleri üzerinden yüklenebilir.

4. Reklamlar

FF BASE uygulamayı desteklemek için reklam gösterir. Güncel sağlayıcılar:

  • Google AdMob (uygulama içi doğrudan App Open reklamları)
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource (geçiş interstitial, native reklamlar ve mediation)
  • LevelPlay üzerinden katılabilecek ağlar (ör. Google AdMob, Unity Ads — müzayede sonucuna göre)

Bu sağlayıcılar, kendi politikalarına ve geçerli onay seçimlerinize göre cihaz, reklam kimliği (izin verilen yerlerde Advertising ID), etkileşim ve tanı bilgilerini işleyebilir.

Yasal olarak gerekli bölgelerde (ör. AEA/BK) reklam için onay / gizlilik seçimleri gösterilebilir (ör. Google UMP). Tercihleriniz kişiselleştirilmiş veya kişiselleştirilmemiş reklamları etkileyebilir.

6. Kullanım amaçları

  • FF BASE’i işletmek ve geliştirmek

  • Reklam göstermek ve mediation’ı desteklemek

Kişisel verinizi tüketici profili olarak satmıyoruz. Supabase, YouTube/Google ve yukarıdaki reklam ortakları, ilgili hizmetleri sunmak için kendi koşulları altında veri işleyebilir.
